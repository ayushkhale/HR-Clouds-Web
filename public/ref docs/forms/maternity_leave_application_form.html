5. STATUTORY PROVISIONS & COMPANY GUIDELINES
Key Provisions under the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017
  1. Benefit Entitlement: Every woman employee who has actually worked in the organization for a period of at least 80 days in the 12 months immediately preceding the date of her expected delivery is entitled to maternity benefit with full average daily wages.
  2. Leave Duration: The maximum period of paid maternity benefit is 26 weeks for up to two surviving children (of which not more than 8 weeks shall precede the expected date of delivery). For female employees with two or more surviving children, the entitlement is 12 weeks.
  3. Prohibition of Employment: No woman employee shall work in any establishment during the six weeks immediately following the day of her delivery or miscarriage. Engaging in any remunerative employment during the leave period shall forfeit benefit rights.
  4. Nursing Breaks: Upon return to duty, every employee who has delivered a child shall be allowed two nursing breaks of prescribed duration in the course of her daily work until the child attains the age of fifteen months.
  5. Work From Home Provision: Where the nature of work assigned allows, the company and the employee may mutually agree to a work-from-home arrangement following the expiry of maternity benefit on terms mutually agreed.
