# HR & Employee Guide: Leave Policy Assignment & Balances (Q&A)

This document explains how Leave Policies are assigned to employees, how leave balances are calculated, and how both HR and employees can view leave balances.

### Assigning Leave Policies

**Q: How do employees get their leave balances?**
Employees receive their leave balances when HR assigns a "Leave Policy Template" to them. Once the template is assigned, the system automatically calculates the employee's quotas and creates their leave balance "wallet" for the year.
*Additional Explanation:* The specific leave types and annual quotas included in the template depend entirely on the organization's policy. For example, one organization might offer 18 days of Privilege Leave and 12 days of Sick Leave, while another might offer 15 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) and 10 days of Casual Leave.

**Q: What happens if an employee joins in the middle of the year? Do they get the full annual leave quota?**
No, the system automatically handles mid-year joiners using "Pro-Rata" calculations.
*Additional Explanation:* If an employee joins in July, the system looks at their Joining Date and calculates exactly how many days are left in the year. It then credits them only a proportionate fraction of the annual quota (rounded to the nearest half-day).

**Q: What if an employee's role changes mid-year and they need a different leave policy?**
HR can simply assign the new policy template to the employee. The system handles "Policy Reassignments" gracefully.
*Additional Explanation:* When reassigned, the system remembers how many leaves the employee has already taken this year; taken leave is never reset. For an upfront leave type, the balance moves by the difference between the old and new annual quotas, pro-rated from 1 January (or from the joining date if the employee joined this year). It is not pro-rated from the reassignment date. Leave types the new policy does not include stop being applicable, but their balances are kept.

### Individual Overrides

**Q: Can HR give a specific employee more (or fewer) leave days than what is in their assigned policy template?**
Yes. While templates are used to assign standard rules across the company, HR has the ability to "Override" individual settings for a specific employee.
*Additional Explanation:* Leave quotas depend on each organization's policies and employment terms. For example, if an employee negotiates an extra 5 days of Casual Leave, HR can go to that specific employee's profile and override their quota (for instance, increasing an entitlement from 10 days to 15 days, or granting 18 days instead of 12) without affecting anyone else assigned to the same template.

**Q: Can HR override whether an employee's leaves carry forward to the next year?**
Yes. In addition to overriding the annual quota, HR can also override individual settings like "Max Carry Forward" for a specific employee (for example, allowing a key employee to carry forward 10 or 18 days even if the organization's default template limit is 5 days).

### Viewing Leave Balances

**Q: Where can an employee check how many leave days they have left?**
Employees can view their own leave balances through the Self-Service portal. The system displays their "Total Accrued" (leaves earned so far), "Total Used" (leaves taken and approved), and their "Current Balance" (leaves available to use).

**Q: Can HR view an employee's leave balance?**
Yes. HR Administrators have access to view the leave balances of any employee in the organization.

**Q: If an employee's policy accrues leave "Monthly", when do they see their balance increase?**
If a leave type is set to Monthly accrual, the employee will see their "Total Accrued" and "Current Balance" slowly increase month by month, rather than getting the full annual amount upfront.

### Leave Assignment Screen (Phase 7)

**Q: How can HR see who is on which leave policy, and who has none?**
Setup › Leave › Leave Assignment lists every active member with their current policy, the date it started, who assigned it, and how many leave types were customised for them. People with no policy are listed too, marked "No policy".
*Additional Explanation:* People who were given a policy before this screen existed show as "on a policy (untracked)", because the system did not record which policy it was. Re-assigning a policy to them makes it tracked.

**Q: What happens if HR assigns the same policy to someone who is already on it?**
Nothing changes. The person keeps any custom rules HR set for them, and their balances are not touched.

**Q: Can HR give a policy to a whole department at once?**
Yes. HR picks the people (by department, location, employment type, job status, or by name) and first sees a preview: who is already on the policy, who will move and from which policy, whose custom rules will be replaced, and who cannot be assigned (for example, people with no joining date on file). Up to 200 people can be assigned in one go; each person is handled separately, so the result shows exactly who was assigned and who was not.

**Q: An employee is leaving. How does HR stop their leave policy?**
HR ends the policy assignment on the employee's last working day. Their balances stay exactly as they are (for the full and final settlement); they cannot apply for leave after that date, and monthly accrual stops. If leave is already booked after that date, it must be cancelled or rejected first.

**Q: Can HR undo a customisation and go back to the policy's numbers?**
Yes. In the Customise dialog, "Revert to the policy default" puts that leave type back on the values the policy gave the person and adjusts the balance accordingly.

**Q: Why does assigning a policy say the employee has no joining date?**
Leave is calculated from the joining date, so the system no longer guesses one. HR can fill in a missing joining date once from the employee's HR fields, then assign the policy.

**Q: Can a policy be deleted while people are on it?**
No. HR first moves those people to another policy; the Leave Policies page shows how many people are on each one.
