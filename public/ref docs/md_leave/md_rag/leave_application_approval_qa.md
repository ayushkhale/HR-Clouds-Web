# HR, Manager & Employee Guide: Applying & Approving Leaves (Q&A)

This document explains the process of applying for leave, how the system routes requests for approval, and how managers process those requests.

### Applying for Leave (Employees)

**Q: How does the system calculate how many days of leave I am using?**
When you select your start and end dates, the system automatically looks at the company calendar. It skips weekends, public holidays, and your specific non-working days. It only counts actual working days.
*Exception:* This can depend on organization to organization. If HR has enabled the "Sandwich Rule" for that specific leave type in your organization's policy, weekends and holidays falling between your leave days will also be counted as leave days.

**Q: Can I apply for a Half-Day?**
Yes. When applying, you can select whether it is a full day or a half-day (First Half or Second Half). The system will only deduct 0.5 days from your balance.

**Q: What happens if I apply for leave, but I don't have enough balance?**
The system will prevent you from submitting the request if it exceeds your balance, unless your organization's policy permits a negative balance.
*Additional Explanation:* Handling of insufficient balances can depend on organization to organization. If your organization's policy allows negative balances (for example, allowing employees to borrow up to 2 or 5 days against future accruals), you can still submit the request within that allowance. Alternatively, if your organization has enabled unpaid leaves (like Leave Without Pay / LWP), the system may automatically split your request by using up your remaining paid balance first and marking the remainder as unpaid.

**Q: If my request is pending manager approval, can I use that same balance for another request?**
No. When you submit a request, the system places a "hold" on those days (often called a phantom hold). Even though the days haven't been permanently deducted yet, you cannot use them for another request.

**Q: Can I apply for leave that spans across two different calendar years (e.g., Dec 28 to Jan 3)?**
No. The system requires leave balances to be tracked accurately per calendar year. You must submit two separate requests: one ending on Dec 31, and a new one starting on Jan 1.

### Approval Routing & Manager Actions

**Q: Who approves my leave request?**
The system automatically routes your request to your direct reporting manager. 

**Q: What if I don't have a manager assigned, or my manager is the CEO?**
If the system cannot find a manager for you, it will "escalate" your request. The request goes into a shared queue where any HR Administrator can review and approve it.

**Q: What happens when a manager approves a leave?**
Two things happen instantly:
1. Your leave balance is permanently deducted.
2. Your attendance record for those dates is marked as "On Leave", so you won't be flagged as absent.

**Q: Can a manager approve a leave if the employee was already marked absent by the system?**
Yes. If an employee was sick and marked absent, and they apply for leave the next day, the manager can approve it. The system will automatically convert the "Absent" mark to "On Leave".

### Cancelling Leaves

**Q: Can I cancel my leave request?**
Yes, but the process depends on the status of your request and the date:
- **If the request is still Pending:** You can cancel it immediately. The hold on your balance is released instantly.
- **If the request is Approved, but the leave date is in the future:** You can cancel it immediately. Your balance is refunded instantly.
- **If the request is Approved, and the leave date has already started/passed:** You cannot cancel it immediately. When you try to cancel, it goes into a "Cancellation Pending" state. Your manager must approve the cancellation before your balance is refunded. This prevents employees from taking leave and then cancelling it later to get their balance back.
