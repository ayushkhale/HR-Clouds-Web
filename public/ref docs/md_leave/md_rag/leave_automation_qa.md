# HR & Employee Guide: Automation, Accruals & Year-End Rollover (Q&A)

This document explains the automated background processes that manage leave balances over time, including monthly accruals and year-end rollovers.

### Monthly Accruals

**Q: How does the system calculate my balance if my policy is set to "Monthly" accrual?**
If your policy specifies monthly accrual, you do not receive your full annual quota upfront. Instead, the system runs an automated process on the 1st of every month to credit your balance.

**Q: Will I get the exact same amount every month?**
The system calculates exactly how many months have passed since your policy started this year, determines your cumulative target, and credits the difference. This prevents tiny decimal rounding errors and ensures that by December, you have received exactly your annual quota.

**Q: What happens if HR changes my quota in the middle of the year?**
The automated monthly process is smart enough to handle mid-year changes. If HR increases your quota, your next monthly accrual will be slightly larger to catch you up to the new target. If HR decreases your quota, the system will never take away leaves you've already used, but it may pause your future accruals until the math balances out.

### Year-End Rollover

**Q: What happens to my unused leaves at the end of the year?**
On January 1st of every year, the system runs a "Year-End Rollover" process. It looks at your final balance from December 31st and applies your policy's "Carry Forward" rules.

**Q: What is "Max Carry Forward"?**
The Max Carry Forward defines the maximum number of unused leave days you are allowed to carry over into the new year. 
*Additional Explanation:* This limit can depend on organization to organization. Some companies enforce a strict "use-it-or-lose-it" rule with 0 carry forward, while others allow carrying over a set number of days (for example, 5, 10, or 18 days). 
For instance, if your organization sets the Max Carry Forward to 5 days:
- If you have 10 days left and the Max Carry Forward is 5, exactly 5 days will be carried into the new year, and the remaining 5 days will "lapse" (expire).
- If you have 3 days left and the Max Carry Forward is 5, all 3 days will be carried into the new year.

**Q: How does the rollover work for "Upfront" vs. "Monthly" policies?**
- **Upfront:** On January 1st, you receive your new annual quota immediately, plus any carry-forward from last year.
- **Monthly:** On January 1st, you receive your carry-forward from last year. Your new quota will then start trickling in month by month.

**Q: What happens if my leave balance is negative at the end of the year?**
Whether an employee can have a negative balance depends on the organization's policy. If your company allows a "Max Negative Balance" (for example, allowing employees to borrow up to 2 or 5 days) and you took more leaves than you accrued, the negative balance will carry forward into the new year. This means you start the new year in "debt" and will have to earn leaves to bring your balance back up to zero. If your organization sets the limit to 0, negative balances are disallowed entirely.

**Q: Does the rollover process affect Comp Offs?**
No. Comp Off balances do not participate in the year-end rollover. Instead, Comp Offs follow their own rolling expiry window based on when you earned them (which depends on your organization's policy, for example, a 30, 60, or 90-day validity window), regardless of the calendar year.
