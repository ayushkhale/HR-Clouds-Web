# Employee, Manager & HR Guide: Holidays, Weekly Offs & Calendar Exceptions (Q&A)

This document provides a comprehensive, stateless guide to Company Holidays, Weekly Off Rules, and Calendar Exceptions in the HRMS, explaining how non-working days are defined, how regional calendars operate, and how working on rest days impacts attendance and compensation.

---

### Company Holidays

**Q: How do Company Holidays work in the HRMS?**
**A:** Company Holidays are dates designated by the organization as official non-working days for which employees receive paid time off without having to apply for leave.

On a designated holiday, employees are not scheduled to work and are not marked absent if they do not clock in.

---

**Q: Can holidays be specific to certain locations, departments, or employment types?**
**A:** Yes. Organizations operating across multiple cities, states, or regions can configure granular holiday calendars:
- **Location-Specific Holidays:** Applicable only to employees assigned to specific branches or offices (for example, regional state holidays or festival holidays).
- **Department-Specific Holidays:** Applicable only to certain operational departments.
- **Employment-Type Holidays:** Specific to full-time staff, interns, or contract personnel.
- **User Inclusions / Exclusions:** HR can explicitly include or exclude specific individual employees from a holiday if their shift or project demands require alternate scheduling.

---

**Q: What is an Optional or Floating Holiday?**
**A:** An Optional Holiday (sometimes called a Restricted Holiday) is a recognized cultural or religious festival that is not an automatic mandatory day off for the entire company.

Employees may choose to observe an optional holiday from a pre-approved list, subject to company policy limits and managerial approval in the Leave module.

---

### Weekly Off Rules (Rest Days)

**Q: What is a Weekly Off Rule in the HRMS?**
**A:** A Weekly Off Rule establishes the standard weekly rest days (non-working days) for employees (for example, Saturday and Sunday for corporate teams, or Sunday only for retail staff).

Weekly off rules ensure that the system does not mark employees absent on their regular rest days.

---

**Q: Can different shifts or departments have different Weekly Off days?**
**A:** Yes. HR Administrators can define distinct weekly off rules for different shifts, departments, locations, or employment types. For example, a customer support team working on a Tuesday-to-Saturday schedule can have Sunday and Monday assigned as their weekly off days.

---

**Q: What happens if multiple Weekly Off rules seem to apply to the same employee?**
**A:** The system evaluates rules using a priority hierarchy. Specific rules (such as a rule linked directly to the employee's assigned shift or department) take precedence over general organization-wide defaults.

---

### Calendar Exceptions (Special Overrides)

**Q: What is a Calendar Exception in the HRMS?**
**A:** A Calendar Exception is an administrative override used by HR to temporarily alter the working or holiday status of a specific calendar date for a group of employees.

There are two primary types of exceptions:
1. **Working Day Exception:** Converts a date that would normally be a holiday or weekly off (such as a Saturday) into a regular working day (for example, to make up for an extended holiday shutdown).
2. **Cancelled Holiday Exception:** Cancels a previously announced public holiday, making it a regular scheduled workday.

When a calendar exception is active, the system treats the date according to the override, expecting standard attendance from affected employees.

---

### Impact on Daily Attendance

**Q: What happens if an employee does not clock in on a Holiday or Weekly Off?**
**A:** The system automatically identifies that the date is a scheduled rest day:
- Your daily attendance status is recorded as "Holiday" or "Weekly Off".
- You are not penalized, and the system will not mark you absent.
- The date does not deduct any balance from your personal leave quotas.

---

**Q: What happens if an employee clocks in and works on a Holiday or Weekly Off?**
**A:** If your job requires you to work on an official holiday or scheduled weekly off:
1. **Marked Present:** When you clock in and complete your hours, your attendance status is marked as "Present".
2. **Effective Hours Counted:** Your working hours are fully counted toward your monthly productive hours.
3. **Compensatory Off Earned:** If your company attendance policy has compensatory off enabled, the system automatically creates an "Earned" Comp Off credit for you, allowing you to claim a paid day off in the future.

---

### Employee Self-Service Views

**Q: How do I view the list of holidays applicable to me?**
**A:** In your Attendance dashboard, navigate to "Holidays" or "Holiday Calendar".

The system displays all upcoming holidays for the selected calendar year that apply specifically to your office location, department, and employment type.

---

**Q: What is the Weekly Calendar view?**
**A:** The Weekly Calendar is an interactive 7-day schedule view accessible from your Attendance dashboard.

It provides an integrated look at your upcoming week:
- Shows your scheduled working shifts and official timings for each day.
- Highlights your scheduled Weekly Offs and upcoming Public Holidays.
- Displays your recorded clock-in/out punches, total hours, and statuses for days already completed.
- Helps you identify any upcoming schedule shifts or rest days at a glance.
