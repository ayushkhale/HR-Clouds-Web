# HRMS User Knowledge Base

Welcome to the comprehensive guide for the HRMS portal. This knowledge base is designed to answer your questions and guide you through all available functionalities in the system. 

To make navigation simple, this document is divided into two primary sections based on user roles:
1. **Part 1: Employee Panel** (Features available to all staff members)
2. **Part 2: Manager & HR Panel** (Administrative and team management features)

---

# Part 1: Employee Panel

The Employee Panel is your personal self-service portal. It allows you to manage your own profile, log your working hours, apply for leaves, and securely access your financial information.

## 1.1 Personal Dashboard
**What is the Employee Dashboard?**
Upon logging in, you are greeted by the Personal Dashboard. It serves as a quick snapshot of your essential HR information, providing immediate access to your daily statistics.

**What details are visible on the dashboard?**
- **Attendance Snapshot:** Your total hours worked today, current clock-in status, and weekly attendance trends.
- **Leave Summary:** A quick view of your remaining balance for Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), and Paid Leave (PL).
- **Quick Links:** Immediate access buttons to download your latest payslip or clock out for the day.

## 1.2 Profile & Directory

### My Profile
**What can you do in the Personal Profile?**
Your profile is the central hub for your identity within the organization. You can view and update:
- **Personal Details:** First name, last name, display name, date of birth, gender, blood group, and marital status.
- **Contact Information:** Mobile phone number, personal email address, current residential address, permanent address, city, state, and pincode.
- **Profile Picture (Avatar):** Upload a professional profile picture that will represent you across the HRMS directory.
- **Employment Information (View-Only):** Review your official records, including your employee code, department, designation (job title), joining date, employment type (Full-time, Part-time, Intern, Contract), current job status (e.g., Confirmed, Probation), reporting manager, and work mode (Remote, On-site, Hybrid).

**Can I change my department or manager?**
No. Employment types, job status, department, and reporting manager are official organizational fields and can only be modified by HR Administrators.

### Company Directory
**What is the Company Directory?**
A searchable organizational chart and contact list. It allows you to easily find colleagues across different departments.

**What information is visible to me?**
You can search for active employees and view their names, profile pictures, departments, designations, and professional work locations. Sensitive personal information (like personal phone numbers, home addresses, or DOBs) of your colleagues is hidden for privacy.

## 1.3 Attendance & Time Tracking

**How do I log my time?**
The HRMS supports multiple methods for clocking in and out, ensuring flexibility:
- **Biometric Devices:** Fingerprint or face recognition at office locations.
- **Web Portal Access:** Secure login from your office desktop or laptop.
- **Mobile App:** Clock in via your smartphone (includes GPS & geofencing support for field staff).

**What can I track?**
- **Breaks:** Explicitly log when you start and end breaks during the workday to ensure accurate total-hour calculations.
- **Overtime & Comp-Offs:** Track your recorded overtime hours and accumulated compensatory time-off balances for working on non-working days.

**What is a Regularization Request?**
If you forget to clock in, miss a punch, or experience a schedule deviation (an "anomaly"), you can submit a "Regularization Request". 
- **Process:** Submit the correct time along with a reason. This request is sent to your reporting manager.
- **Approval:** Once your manager approves, your official attendance log is corrected. If rejected, the original time log remains. You can also cancel the request before the manager reviews it.

## 1.4 Leave Management

**What types of leave can I apply for?**
Depending on company policy, you can apply for Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), Paid/Earned Leave (PL/EL), or Maternity/Paternity Leave. You can request either a full day or a half day.

**How does the application process work?**
1. **Check Balances:** Your dashboard displays real-time balances for all assigned leave types.
2. **Apply:** Select the dates and leave type. The system will preview the impact on your balance.
3. **Upload Documents:** For specific scenarios (like taking Sick Leave for more than a threshold of days), the system will require you to upload a supporting medical certificate.
4. **Approval:** Your request is routed to your manager. Your leave balance is temporarily held until approved or rejected.

**Can I cancel a leave?**
Yes, you can withdraw a leave request if your plans change, provided the leave hasn't already been consumed or processed in a past payroll cycle.

## 1.5 Payroll & Salary

**How do I access my salary information?**
The Payroll module provides secure, transparent access to your compensation records.
- **Payslips:** View and download your detailed monthly payslips in PDF format. Payslips display your gross pay, net payout, and an itemized list of all deductions.
- **Salary Structure:** Review your active compensation breakdown, including fixed components like Basic Pay, HRA, and Special Allowances.
- **Bank Details:** Manage and update the bank account details where your salary is deposited.

**What else can I track?**
- **Bonuses:** Track performance bonuses, incentives, or manual salary adjustments applied to your pay.
- **Loans & Advances:** If you have a company-provided loan, you can view the active status, EMI schedules, and pending balance.
- **Tax & Compliance:** Access tax deduction summaries (TDS) and download Form 16 for your income tax filings.

# Part 2: Manager & HR Panel

The Manager & HR Panel provides elevated privileges for team leaders, department heads, and HR administrators to oversee their workforce, enforce policies, and manage organizational operations.

## 2.1 Manager & HR Dashboard
**What is the Manager Dashboard?**
A high-level command center designed for oversight. 
- **Widgets & Cards:** Instantly view today's attendance rate (e.g., 95% present), total team size, and the number of employees working remotely versus on-site.
- **Alerts:** Highlights pending leave requests and regularization requests that require immediate manager approval.

## 2.2 Employee Management

**What can Managers do?**
Managers can view the complete, detailed profiles of their direct reports. If a team member is unable to update their own contact information, the manager can step in and edit the personal profile fields on their behalf.

**What advanced capabilities do HR Administrators have?**
HR Administrators manage the official employment lifecycle across the entire organization:
- **Change Employment Status:** Update an employee's job status (e.g., moving an employee from "Probation" to "Confirmed", or marking them as "Terminated" or on "Notice Period").
- **Department Transfers:** Officially transfer an employee from one department or location to another, thereby updating their reporting structure.
- **Offboarding & Deactivation:** When an employee leaves, HR can securely deactivate or softly delete their profile. This removes their access and hides them from the Company Directory, while preserving their historical records for auditing purposes.

## 2.3 Attendance Administration

**How do managers handle team attendance?**
- **Review Logs:** View a tabular breakdown of the team's daily in-times, out-times, total hours, and status (Present/Late/Absent).
- **Regularization Approvals:** Managers receive notifications for team members' missed punches. They can review the context and approve or reject the time correction.
- **Manual Adjustments (HR):** HR administrators can manually mark employees as absent, edit timesheets, or configure organizational shift timings and rules.

## 2.4 Leave Approvals & Administration

**How do managers process leave requests?**
Managers see a dedicated dashboard of pending leave requests for their team. 
- They can view the requested dates, the reason provided, and any attached medical documents.
- Managers can assess the workload impact and either approve or reject the request, optionally leaving a remark.

**How does HR configure leave policies?**
- **Policy Assignment:** HR can create new leave types, build policy templates (e.g., "Standard Tier", "Executive Tier"), and assign specific policies to new employees.
- **Overrides & Automation:** HR can override standard configurations for special cases (e.g., manually granting extra leave) and can manually trigger organizational monthly accruals or year-end leave rollovers.

## 2.5 Payroll Processing (HR Only)

**How is the organization's payroll managed?**
Payroll processing is restricted to authorized HR and Finance administrators.
- **Design Salary Structures:** HR creates templates for compensation bands and dynamically assigns them to employees.
- **Adjustments & Deductions:** Before running payroll, HR can bulk-upload or manually enter ad-hoc bonuses, incentives, or specific deductions.
- **Loan Management:** Grant employee loans and configure automated EMI deductions that tie directly into the monthly payslip.
- **Generate Payroll Run:** HR executes the monthly payroll cycle. The system automatically cross-references the employee's salary structure with their attendance data (deducting unpaid absences) and active loans to calculate the final net payout. 
- **Final Approval:** Once the payroll run completes the approval workflow and is marked as "Paid", individual payslips are automatically generated and made visible to employees on their personal dashboards.
