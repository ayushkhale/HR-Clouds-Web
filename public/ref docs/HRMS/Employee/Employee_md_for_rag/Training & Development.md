# Training & Development — Availability Status for the Employee Role

**Document Information**
Role: Employee · Availability: Not currently available · Last verified: 2026-09-10

> **Documentation basis:** This document describes verified system behavior for the
> Employee role in the Dakshi HRMS platform. At the time of writing, the platform is
> delivered as a backend API with a reference dashboard prototype; a full production
> user interface is not yet released. Where a screen or navigation path is described,
> it reflects the reference Employee Dashboard prototype. Where no screen exists, this
> document describes what the system does, not where to click.
> **Last verified against source:** 2026-09-10

## 1. Current Availability of Training & Development

**Status: Not currently available.** There is no training or development capability for the Employee role in the platform today. An employee cannot access training modules, enroll in courses, track learning progress, or download training certificates, because no training and development feature is implemented.

## 2. What This Means for an Employee Today

An employee has no course library, no assigned-training list, no progress tracker, no training calendar, and no certificate store in the platform. Learning and development activities are organized through whatever process an employee's organization uses outside the platform.

## 3. Capabilities Described in Planning Documents but Not Implemented

The following were described in a requirements document and are not available to an employee:

- Viewing and accessing assigned training modules (onboarding, soft skills, compliance, leadership)
- Skill development programs and personalized learning suggestions
- Progress and completion tracking, assessment scores, and reminders
- Post-training feedback and downloadable certificates
- A training calendar with self-registration for sessions, webinars, or workshops
- Training notifications and reminders
- Gamification, points, badges, and leaderboards

## 4. Where Related Needs Are Handled Today

There is no in-platform training or development feature. An employee should follow their organization's own learning and development process outside the platform.

## 5. Common Employee Questions About Training & Development

### Where do I find my assigned training or courses?
Assigned training and courses are not available in the platform. There is no training and development feature. Your organization will tell you how it delivers training.

### How do I enroll in a training session or webinar?
There is no training calendar or enrollment feature in the platform. Register for sessions through whatever process your organization provides.

### How do I track my course progress or assessment scores?
Course progress tracking is not available in the platform, because no training feature is implemented.

### Where can I download my training certificates?
The platform does not store or issue training certificates, because no training and development feature exists. Obtain certificates through your organization's own process.

### Are there points, badges, or a leaderboard for training?
No. Gamification for training is not available in the platform, because no training feature is implemented.
