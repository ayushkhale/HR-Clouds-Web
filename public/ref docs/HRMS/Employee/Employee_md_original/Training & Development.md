# Training & Development

The Training & Development module under the HRMS Employee Panel is designed to
enhance employee skills, improve performance, and support continuous professional
growth. This section ensures that learning and development initiatives are streamlined,
measurable, and accessible to every employee.
Functional Features:

### 1. Assigned Training Modules

- View and access training modules assigned by HR or reporting managers.
- Includes onboarding tutorials, soft skills, compliance training, domain-specific

programs, and leadership development tracks.
- Training is available in multiple formats: video, PDF, interactive quizzes, or SCORM

packages.

### 2. Skill Development Programs

- Explore organization-curated or third-party skill development programs integrated

into the HRMS.
- Tracks progress across skills, certifications, and proficiency levels.
- Personalized suggestions based on job role, department goals, or career path.

### 3. Progress & Completion Tracking

- Real-time progress dashboard for each employee.
- Track module-wise completion percentage, time spent, and assessment

performance.
- Auto-generated reminders for incomplete courses or deadlines.

### 4. Feedback & Certification

- Submit post-training feedback for content quality, trainer effectiveness, and

delivery.
- View/download training certificates upon successful completion.
- Certifications are stored in the employee's profile for appraisal or compliance

purposes.

### 5. Training Calendar & Registration

- Browse upcoming training sessions, webinars, and workshops via an interactive

calendar.
- Filter sessions by location, department, topic, or trainer.
- Self-registration with approval flow if required.
- Integration with internal or external video conferencing tools (Zoom, Google Meet,

MS Teams).

### 6. Notification & Reminders

- Automated alerts for new trainings, deadlines, updates, and certificate availability.
- Customizable notification preferences via email, SMS, or HRMS dashboard.

### 7. Gamification & Leaderboard (Optional Feature)

- Earn points and badges for completing training milestones.
- View departmental or organizational leaderboards to foster healthy competition.
