Document Management Module – Features & Functionalities
The Document Management system in the Employee Panel of the HRMS is a secure,
centralized platform enabling seamless handling of all essential HR documents. It
simplifies access, uploading, and acknowledgment of key documents while ensuring
compliance and traceability.

## Core Functionalities

### 1. Centralized Access to HR Documents

  - View and download essential HR documents including:
  - Appointment Letters
  - Employment Contracts
  - NDAs (Non-Disclosure Agreements)
  - Company Policies (e.g., Leave, Travel, Code of Conduct)
  - Promotion or Increment Letters
  - Smart categorization by document type or department

### 2. Employee Document Uploads

  - Upload personal or statutory documents for HR reference, such as:
  - PAN Card, Aadhar Card
  - Educational Certificates
  - Experience Letters
  - Medical Certificates or Fitness Proof
  - HR gets real-time notifications for new uploads

### 3. Digital Signature & Acknowledgment Integration

  - Secure e-signature capability on offer letters, NDAs, or updates
  - Acknowledgment tracking for policy compliance
  - Legal validation with audit trail (timestamp, IP, user ID)

### 4. Automated Document Reminders

  - Alerts for document expiry or renewal (e.g., ID proof, licenses)
  - Reminder emails/SMS for pending document acknowledgments

### 5. Downloadable Templates & Forms

  - HR-provided templates (e.g., reimbursement forms, leave application

formats)
  - Easy-to-fill, upload-ready formats for standard use cases

### 6. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

  - Access permissions based on role (employee, HR, manager)
  - Confidentiality maintained for sensitive HR documents
