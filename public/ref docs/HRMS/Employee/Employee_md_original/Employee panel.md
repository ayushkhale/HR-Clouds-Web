Employee Panel – Your HRMS Hub for Everything Work-Related
The Employee Panel in our HRMS platform is a comprehensive, user-friendly interface
designed to empower employees with full control over their professional data, streamline
communication, and enhance productivity. From leave applications to payslip downloads,
every HR function is just a few clicks away.

## Key Features of the Employee Panel

## Personal Dashboard

- Quick overview of attendance, leaves, upcoming holidays, and tasks
- Real-time notifications and announcements
- Customizable widgets for a personalized experience

## Profile Management

- View and update personal details, contact info, and emergency contacts
- Upload documents like ID proofs, resumes, and certifications
- Track employment history, promotions, and role changes

## Attendance & Time Tracking

- Clock in/out with biometric, web, or mobile access
- View daily, weekly, or monthly attendance reports
- Request regularizations for missed punches or incorrect time logs

## Leave Management

- Apply for different types of leaves (casual, sick, paid, etc.)
- Track leave balance, approval status, and history
- Integrated holiday calendar and auto leave policy enforcement

## Payroll & Salary

- Access to payslips, salary breakup, and tax details
- Download monthly/annual payslips and Form 16
- View bonus, deductions, reimbursements, and loan details

## Performance & Appraisal

- View performance reviews, KPIs, and manager feedback
- Submit self-assessments and 360° feedback
- Track goal progress and appraisal cycles

## Training & Development

- Browse assigned training modules and skill development programs
- Track course completion, feedback, and certification
- Register for upcoming training sessions and webinars

## Document Management

- Access important HR documents (e.g., company policies, NDAs, offer letters)
- Upload personal files for HR reference
- Digital signatures and document acknowledgment support

## Expense & Reimbursement

- Submit expense claims with receipts and category tagging
- Track status of reimbursements in real time
- Multi-currency support and department-wise approvals

## Communication & Collaboration

- Internal chat and message system
- Company-wide announcements and targeted notifications
- Survey participation, suggestion box, and feedback forms

## Helpdesk / Support Tickets

- Raise service requests for HR, IT, or Admin support
- Track ticket status and communicate with support team
- Categorized query management and SLA tracking

## Settings & Preferences

- Manage notification preferences
- Set working hours, shift preferences, and remote work status
- Multi-language and accessibility settings

## Mobile Friendly & Secure

- Available on web, iOS, and Android platforms
- Encrypted data protection and multi-factor authentication
- GDPR and labor law compliant

## Why Employees Love It

- No paperwork, no HR queues – complete self-service
- Transparent, real-time access to all employment data
- Enhances work-life balance and HR engagement
