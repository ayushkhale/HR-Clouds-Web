HRMS Employee Panel – Expense & Reimbursement Module
The Expense & Reimbursement feature under the HRMS Employee Panel is designed to
streamline financial workflows by allowing employees to easily manage and submit their
work-related expenses. This module promotes transparency, accountability, and efficiency
in handling reimbursements.

## Key Functions & Features

### 1. Submit Expense Claims

- Employees can initiate reimbursement requests for eligible expenses such as travel,

food, accommodation, communication, or miscellaneous costs.
- Upload supporting documents like receipts, invoices, or bills in PDF/JPEG/PNG

format.
- Tag each expense under predefined categories (e.g., Travel, Meals,

Accommodation).
- Add custom remarks to explain specific charges.

### 2. Real-Time Reimbursement Tracking

- Live status updates available at each stage of the reimbursement process:

Submitted → Under Review → Approved/Rejected → Processed
- Notifications via email and in-app alerts for each status update.
- Option to view historical records of all past reimbursement claims and their

statuses.

### 3. Multi-Currency Support

- Supports expense entries in multiple currencies, ideal for global teams or

international travel.
- Automated currency conversion to the employee's home currency based on real-

time exchange rates.
- Finance teams can process reimbursements in local currency as per company

policy.

### 4. Department-Wise Approval Workflow

- Customizable workflows to route expense claims through the correct approval

chain.
- Department Heads or Team Leads receive notifications for pending approvals.
- Automatic escalation of unapproved claims after a predefined time period.

### 5. Policy Compliance & Automation

- Built-in validation rules aligned with company reimbursement policies.
- Auto-flagging of non-compliant or duplicate claims.
- Automated limits on claim amounts based on employee role/grade.

### 6. Reports & Audit Logs

- Generate monthly/quarterly reports of individual or department-level

reimbursements.
- Full audit trail for each claim: who submitted, who reviewed, who approved.
- Downloadable summary reports for tax or accounting purposes.

## Benefits

- Faster processing time and improved employee satisfaction.
- Reduced manual errors and fraud with automated checks.
- Ensures compliance with internal and tax regulations.
