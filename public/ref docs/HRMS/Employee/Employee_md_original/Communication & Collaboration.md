# Communication & Collaboration

## Overview

The Communication & Collaboration module in the HRMS Employee Panel is designed to
promote smooth and structured communication across teams, departments, and
management. It bridges the gap between employees and HR while encouraging active
participation, feedback, and transparency within the organization.
## Key Functionalities

### 1. Internal Chat and Messaging System

- One-on-One Chats: Secure, real-time messaging between employees.
- Group Chats: Department-based or project-specific groups for faster coordination.
- Message History: Access to past chats for reference and record.
- File Sharing: Attach and share documents, images, and links instantly within chats.

### 2. Company-wide Announcements and Notifications

- Bulletin Board: Displays all official announcements such as policy updates,

holidays, events, etc.
- Push Notifications: Instant alerts sent to desktop or mobile devices to ensure no

important message is missed.
- Targeted Messaging: Segment-based communication (e.g., only Sales team or

Remote staff) for relevant messaging.

### 3. Surveys, Suggestion Box, and Feedback Mechanisms

- Pulse Surveys: Quick, periodic surveys to check employee satisfaction, stress

level, and engagement.
- Anonymous Feedback: Employees can submit their suggestions or concerns

confidentially.
- Suggestion Box: Ongoing open forum to propose new ideas, vote on others'

suggestions, and see which ideas get implemented.
- Feedback Forms: Structured forms to review company initiatives, trainings, events,
  - policies.
