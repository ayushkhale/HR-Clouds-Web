Employee Panel – Core Functionalities of HRMS Services
The Employee Panel is the heart of the HRMS (Human Resource Management System),
empowering your workforce with real-time access to their personal, professional, and
administrative information—anytime, anywhere. Designed for ease of use and productivity,
  - r HRMS transforms traditional HR functions into an automated and transparent

experience.

## Employee Profile Management

- View and update personal information (contact, bank details, address, emergency

contact, etc.)
- Maintain educational qualifications and professional experience
- Upload and manage essential documents (ID proofs, certificates, etc.)

## Attendance & Leave Management

- Real-time attendance tracking with geotagging and facial recognition (if enabled)
- Apply, view, and track leave requests
- View leave balance, holiday calendar, and leave policies

## Payroll & Salary Slip Access

- Access monthly salary slips and tax statements
- Download Form 16 and IT declarations
- Salary breakdown and CTC transparency

## Performance & Appraisal System

- View KRAs/OKRs and performance targets
- Submit self-appraisals and view manager feedback
- Access appraisal history and performance ratings

## Announcements & Notices

- Stay updated with HR policies, circulars, and announcements
- Company-wide notices and personalized messages

## Helpdesk & Support

- Raise service tickets for HR-related queries
- Track ticket status and response time
- Escalation matrix visibility

## Training & Development

- View upcoming training sessions
- Access e-learning modules and certification tracking
- Feedback system for completed training programs

## Team Directory & Collaboration

- View department/team structure and reporting hierarchy
- Access colleagues' contact and professional details
- Internal chat/messaging (if integrated)

## Travel & Reimbursement

- Apply for travel approvals and advances
- Submit expense reports with receipt uploads
- Track reimbursement status and history

## Mobile Friendly & Secure

Our HRMS is built with a mobile-first approach and enterprise-grade security, ensuring
seamless and protected access for your workforce, whether in-office or on the move.

## Cross-Platform Access

- Fully optimized for Web, iOS, and Android
- Lightweight mobile UI for quick navigation
- Instant notifications for approvals, reminders, and alerts

## Data Security & Compliance

- 256-bit SSL encryption for secure communication
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for enhanced login security
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensuring employees only see what they are

authorized to view

## Global Compliance Standards

- GDPR compliant data handling for global workforce
- Adheres to labor laws, including wage protection, employee privacy, and grievance

redressal norms
- Regular data backups and disaster recovery mechanisms in place
