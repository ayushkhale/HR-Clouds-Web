# Core Functions of Payroll & Salary Module

### 1. Payslip Access and Downloads

  - View and download monthly payslips in PDF format.
  - Access annual salary slips with cumulative breakdown.
  - Review previous months’ salary history for personal record-keeping or

verification.

### 2. Detailed Salary Structure

  - Transparent view of basic pay, HRA, special allowances, and performance-

based components.
  - Track monthly bonuses, incentives, and overtime benefits.
  - Access to breakup of fixed and variable components.

### 3. Tax Information and Compliance

  - Real-time visibility into monthly and annual tax deductions (TDS).
  - Access to investment declaration status and tax saving projections.
  - Download Form 16 for income tax filing.

### 4. Deductions & Reimbursements

  - Track deductions for PF, ESI, professional tax, etc.
  - Status of reimbursements like travel, phone bills, internet, etc.
  - Reimbursement claim submission and tracking option.

### 5. Loan & Advance Management

  - View details of company-provided loans or salary advances.
  - EMI schedule and pending balance tracking.
  - Direct deduction status from salary.

### 6. Bonus & Incentive Details

  - Check earned and upcoming bonuses.
  - Incentive structure linked with performance metrics.

## Benefits for Employees

- Easy access to salary and tax-related documents for financial planning.
- Reduces dependency on HR by enabling self-service.
- Enhanced transparency in compensation and deductions.
- Ensures compliance with statutory regulations and financial reporting.
