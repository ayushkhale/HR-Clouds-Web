Key Functions Under Profile Management:

### 1. View & Update Personal Information

- Access complete personal profile details including name, date of birth, gender,

marital status, and nationality.
- Edit and update contact details such as:
  - Permanent and current address
  - Phone number (mobile and alternate)
  - Official and personal email IDs

### 2. Emergency Contact Management

- Add or update emergency contact information.
- Define the relationship, phone number, and address of emergency contacts.
- Option to mark a primary emergency contact.

### 3. Document Upload & Management

- Upload and store important documents such as:
  - Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Passport
  - Educational certificates
  - Professional certifications
  - Updated resumes
  - Experience letters or joining documents
- System auto-validates document format and alerts for expiry (if applicable).

### 4. Employment History & Role Tracking

- View complete employment lifecycle within the organization:
  - Date of joining
  - Past roles and departments
  - Promotions or designations held
  - Transfers across branches or locations
  - Reporting manager history
- Generate a downloadable employment history report.

### 5. Profile Picture Upload

- Upload or update a professional profile picture to be used across internal tools

and portals.

### 6. HR Approval Workflow

- Changes made by the employee (such as contact updates or document uploads)

are sent for HR/admin approval before final submission.

### 7. Alerts and Notifications

- Receive reminders and alerts for:
  - Missing profile details
  - Document expiry or renewal (e.g., ID proof, visa)
  - Profile completion status
