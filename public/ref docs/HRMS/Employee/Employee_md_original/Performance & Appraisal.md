# Performance & Appraisal

## Overview

The Performance & Appraisal module in the HRMS Employee Panel is designed to
streamline the performance management process, ensuring transparency, continuous
feedback, and data-driven evaluations. It empowers employees to stay aligned with their
goals, understand expectations, and grow through regular feedback.
## Key Functionalities

### 1. Performance Review Dashboard

- View past and current performance appraisal results
- Access department-wise and individual performance metrics
- View historical trends and improvement areas
- Personalized KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) set by the reporting manager or HR

### 2. KPI & Goal Management

- Track assigned KPIs and associated weightage
- Monitor progress towards individual, team, and company goals
- View due dates and milestones for goal achievement
- Automatic reminders for goal deadlines

### 3. Manager & Peer Feedback

- Access feedback provided by direct managers or team leads
- View 360-degree feedback from peers, subordinates, and cross-functional teams
- Highlighted strengths and improvement suggestions
- Summary reports of periodic feedback sessions

### 4. Self-Assessment Portal

- Submit self-evaluations for each appraisal cycle
- Rate performance against assigned KPIs and goals
- Provide supporting achievements and qualitative comments
- Track submission status and receive acknowledgment

### 5. Appraisal Cycle Tracker

- View ongoing, upcoming, and past appraisal cycles
- Automated notifications and timeline tracking
- Status of appraisal (Pending, Under Review, Completed)
- Access historical appraisal reports for reference

### 6. Recognition & Ratings Summary

- View rating bands and justification notes
- Download performance reports for personal use
- Performance grading mapped to compensation/bonus models
- Links to internal rewards and recognition programs

### 7. Dispute or Feedback Loop (Optional Feature)

- Raise queries or disputes on submitted appraisals
- Track resolution status with HR or reviewer
- Add clarifications, achievements missed, or additional evidence
## Benefits for Employees

- Clarity on expectations and accountability
- Encourages open and structured communication
- Promotes a culture of feedback and improvement
- Aligns personal growth with organizational objectives
