# Attendance & Time Tracking Module

The Attendance & Time Tracking section of the HRMS Employee Panel enables
seamless, accurate, and real-time monitoring of employee presence, working hours, and
attendance behavior. It empowers employees to self-manage time-related functions
while giving HR and managers full transparency and control.
Key Features and Functions:

### 1. Clock In/Out Flexibility

- Employees can record their work start and end times using multiple modes:
  - Biometric Devices (fingerprint/face recognition)
  - Web Portal Access (using secure login from office systems)
  - Mobile App Access (with GPS & geofencing support for field staff)
- Ensures real-time capture and synchronization of data across all devices.

### 2. Attendance Dashboard

- Personal dashboard view for employees showing:
  - Daily, Weekly, and Monthly Attendance Summary
  - Total Hours Worked
  - Late Arrivals / Early Departures
  - Working Days, Absences, and Holidays
- Color-coded visualization for easy understanding of attendance trends.

### 3. Regularization Request

- Employees can raise requests for:
  - Missed Punches
  - Incorrect In/Out Times
  - Half-day or Full-day Adjustments
- Requests are routed to respective Reporting Managers or HR for approval, with

tracking of status (pending/approved/rejected).
- System logs all changes for audit trail and transparency.

### 4. Real-Time Alerts and Notifications

- Automatic alerts for:
  - Missed attendance logs
  - Approval pending notifications
  - Shift changes or work-hour anomalies
- Ensures accountability and promotes punctuality.

### 5. Integration with Payroll & Leave

- Accurate attendance data is automatically pushed to:
  - Payroll Module – to calculate pay based on present days and working

hours.
  - Leave Management Module – to deduct or regularize leaves as needed.
- Minimizes manual interventions and errors in salary processing.

### 6. Compliance & Policy Control

- Built-in support for organization-specific attendance policies:
  - Grace time, late coming rules
  - Overtime calculation
  - Weekly offs and holiday rules
- Customizable shift rosters and auto-shift detection available.
