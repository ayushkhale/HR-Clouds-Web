# **HRMS Manager Panel – Full Access Dashboard** 

# **Access Level: Manager (Full Access)** 

This panel provides full administrative access to employee records, payroll, performance, and company HR settings for managerial oversight. 

# **Dashboard Overview** 

- Key HR metrics (Headcount, Attrition Rate, Attendance Summary) 

- Announcements and Alerts 

- Quick Links (Leave Approvals, Interviews Today, Pending Requests) 

# **Employee Management** 

- View/Edit All Employee Profiles 

- Add/Remove Employees 

- Upload Documents (Offer Letters, ID Proof, etc.) 

- Assign/Change Departments, Roles, and Reporting Managers 

- Manage Employee Status (Active, On Leave, Resigned, Terminated) 

# **Attendance & Leave Management** 

- View Daily/Monthly Attendance Logs 

- Approve/Reject Leave Requests 

- Configure Shift Timings & Holidays 

- Import/Export Attendance Data 

- Track Remote/WFH Logs 

# **Payroll & Compensation** 

- Generate Monthly Payroll 

- View/Modify Salary Structures 

- Process Bonuses, Incentives, and Deductions 

- Manage Reimbursements and Benefits 

- Download Salary Reports & Payslips 

# **Performance Management** 

- Set & Track OKRs / KPIs 

- Schedule & Record Appraisals 

- View Performance Reviews and Feedback 

- Generate Performance Reports 

- Identify Training Needs 

# **Recruitment Management** 

- Create and Post Job Openings 

- Schedule Interviews and Assign Panels 

- Track Candidate Progress 

- Manage Offer Letters & Onboarding Documents 

# **System & HR Settings** 

- Create/Modify Roles & Permissions 

- Set Leave Policies and Holiday Calendars 

- Configure Payroll Rules & Tax Settings 

- Manage Departments, Locations, and Designations 

- Data Backup & Export 

# **Reports & Analytics** 

- HR Reports: Headcount, Turnover, Leave, Payroll 

- Custom Report Builder 

- Export to Excel/PDF 

- Automated Monthly Reports 

# **Notifications & Alerts** 

- Email & In-System Alerts for: 

   - Leave Requests 

   - Attendance Exceptions 

   - Payroll Discrepancies 

   - Birthdays & Work Anniversaries 

# **Audit Logs & History** 

- View All Actions Performed by Managers 

- Track Logins, Changes, and Approvals 

- Generate Audit Reports for Compliance
