# **Reports & Analytics** 

# **1. HR Reports** 

Provide deep insights into workforce dynamics with automated, up-to-date reporting: 

- **Headcount & Demographics** – Real-time total employees with filters for department, location, employment type (full-time, part-time, contract). 

- **Turnover & Retention** – Monthly/yearly attrition rates, new hires vs. exits, tenure analysis, reasons for leaving. 

- **Leave Management** – Visual leave balances, types (sick, vacation), approvals pipeline, and trends over time. 

- **Payroll Overview** – Summary of pay runs: gross/net amounts, allowances, deductions, bulk flags for anomalies, integration with payslips. 

Tip: Present these through interactive dashboard widgets alongside tabular data for easy patterns and exceptions spotting.
