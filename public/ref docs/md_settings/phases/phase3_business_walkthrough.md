## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose of Phase 3

Welcome to the **Organization Settings Hub (Phase 3)** user guide.

In **Phase 1**, the Organization Settings Hub established a centralized **Discovery and Exploration Center**. It brought transparency to the workplace by allowing HR leaders and department managers to view all 138 organizational settings across 26 categories in one place, compare active values side-by-side with recommended factory defaults, and inspect cross-module company policies.

In **Phase 2**, the Settings Hub evolved into an active **Configuration and Control Center**. Authorized HR administrators gained the power to adjust company-wide policies in place, execute one-click factory resets, collaborate without overwriting colleagues' changes through collaborative safety locks, and protect sensitive rules with mandatory business justifications and high-risk confirmation shields.

**Phase 3 completes the enterprise lifecycle by introducing the Unified Settings Change History and Accountability Ledger.**

As companies grow and multiple administrators manage organizational rules—such as payroll cutoff days, tax deduction percentages, document retention timelines, corporate branding, and billing notifications—answering critical operational and audit questions becomes essential:
* *Who changed this policy?*
* *When was the change made?*
* *What was the exact value before the update, and what did it become?*
* *What business justification or reason was given?*
* *Did the change happen through the Settings Hub, a company profile screen, or an automated system process?*

Phase 3 unites every policy modification across the entire HRMS into a single, chronological, tamper-proof audit timeline. Whether an adjustment touched statutory compliance, employee document policies, salary disbursement dates, or executive billing contacts, HR administrators can audit, filter, search, and verify every historical change from a single command screen.

---

### What is Delivered in Phase 3?

* **One Unified Audit Timeline:** A single, company-wide change history stream covering all 138 organizational settings across 26 categories. No need to check multiple separate audit logs in different modules.
* **Granular Setting-by-Setting Tracking:** Every individual setting modification is recorded as an independent, readable entry showing the exact previous value and the updated value.
* **Full Actor & Role Accountability:** Every change identifies the team member who made it by full name and administrative role. If a modification was generated by a scheduled background process, it is clearly designated as an automated system action.
* **Permanent Justification Capture:** Business justifications and explanations entered during Phase 2 high-risk updates are permanently preserved and displayed alongside the recorded change.
* **Multi-Dimensional Search & Filtering:** Filter audit history by settings category, specific setting name, administrator name, date range, or update channel to locate relevant records in seconds.
* **Noise-Free, Clean Records:** If an administrator opens a settings category and saves without making any changes, the system intelligently skips recording duplicate or empty entries, keeping your audit ledger pristine.
* **Multi-Door Attribution:** Whether a company setting is updated inside the central Settings Hub or adjusted from a dedicated domain screen (such as the Company Profile page), the audit ledger automatically identifies the channel through which the change arrived.
* **Resilient Historical Availability:** If an older archive category is temporarily undergoing data maintenance, the system still displays all remaining available history and provides a helpful status message without interrupting your work.
* **Strict Privacy & Role Separation:** Access to the audit timeline is granted exclusively to authorized HR Administrators. People Managers, general employees, and external platform personnel remain strictly locked out to protect corporate policy confidentiality.

## 4. Core Phase 3 Features Explained

### Feature 1: The Unified Settings Change History Timeline

#### What is it?
A chronological, interactive audit timeline that records every company configuration update. Every entry details exactly which setting changed, who made the change, when it took place, what the setting was before, what it became, and why it was modified.

#### Who can use it?
Authorized **HR Administrators**.

#### Why does it exist?
In the past, understanding why a company policy shifted required contacting colleagues, digging through disparate system logs, or submitting support tickets. The Change History timeline consolidates all policy history into one transparent, self-service dashboard.

#### Where can the user access it?
1. Open the main navigation menu and select **Settings** (or **Organization Settings**).
2. Inside the Settings Hub, click on the **Change History** tab located in the top navigation bar alongside *All Settings* and *Categories*.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ORGANIZATION SETTINGS HUB                                                             │
│  [ All Settings ]   [ Categories ]   [ Change History ★ ]                              │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Step-by-Step Workflow
1. Navigate to **Settings > Change History**.
2. The system automatically loads your organization's most recent setting updates in reverse chronological order (newest first).
3. Review the high-level summary cards showing the setting name, category, administrator name, and timestamp.
4. Click on any history card to view the detailed side-by-side comparison:
   * **Previous Value:** The setting value immediately prior to the update.
   * **New Value:** The setting value that took effect after saving.
   * **Business Reason:** The explanation provided by the administrator (if applicable).
   * **Update Channel:** Where the update was submitted from (e.g. Settings Hub or Company Profile).
5. Scroll down to browse earlier historical pages smoothly.

### Feature 2: Targeted Search & Multi-Dimensional Filters

#### What is it?
A smart filtering panel that lets you narrow down thousands of audit records by specific business criteria.

#### Who can use it?
Authorized **HR Administrators**.

#### Why does it exist?
During annual financial audits, internal compliance checks, or operational reviews, administrators need to locate specific adjustments quickly without browsing through hundreds of unrelated entries.

#### Available Filter Options

| Filter Control | What It Does | Example Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category** | Filters history by a specific settings group. | Review all changes made under *Payroll Calendar* or *Corporate Letterhead*. |
| **Specific Setting** | Focuses on a single configuration rule. | Inspect the historical changes made to *Pay Day* or *PF Employee Rate*. |
| **Team Member / Administrator** | Filters changes made by a specific colleague. | Review all updates submitted by a newly onboarded HR specialist. |
| **Date Range (From / To)** | Limits the timeline to a specific calendar window. | Review all configuration updates executed during the last fiscal quarter. |
| **Update Channel** | Filters by entry point: Settings Hub, Module Screen, or System. | Identify automated system updates versus manual administrative changes. |
| **Entries Per Page** | Adjusts the number of records displayed per view (1 to 100). | Set to 50 or 100 entries for comprehensive report reviewing. |

#### Filter Validation Rules
* **Valid Date Sequence:** The "To" date must not be earlier than the "From" date. If an inverted range is entered, the system alerts you and prompts for a valid date window.
* **Category and Setting Harmony:** If you select both a Category filter and a Specific Setting filter, the selected setting must belong to that category. If they do not match, the system alerts you so you can adjust your selection.

---

### Feature 3: Full Audit Coverage for Company Notification Settings

#### What is it?
Closes the historical audit gap for organization-level notification rules. Specifically, modifications to:
1. **Billing & Invoice Notification Email Recipients**
2. **Subscription Reminder Lead Days (Advance Notice Window)**

#### Why does it exist?
Previously, changes to company notification contacts were stored directly on the company profile without a dedicated change history record. Phase 3 provides full audit tracking for these settings regardless of where they are updated:
* If updated inside the **Settings Hub** (*Billing & Invoices > Notifications*), it is recorded with the channel marked as `Settings Hub`.
* If updated inside the **Company Profile** screen (*Organization > Profile*), it is recorded with the channel marked as `Company Profile Screen`.

Both doors lead to the same comprehensive audit ledger, ensuring complete accountability.

---

### Feature 4: Noise-Free, Clean Records (Smart Diffing)

#### What is it?
An intelligent change-detection mechanism that prevents unnecessary audit clutter.

#### How It Works for You:
1. **No-Op Change Elimination:** If an administrator opens a category, clicks *Save*, but did not actually change any values, the system does not create an audit record. Your history log only contains actual, meaningful modifications.
2. **Accurate Structured Settings:** Complex configuration fields—such as lists of eligible leave types for final settlement or decimal contribution rates—are evaluated intelligently so that formatting differences or list reordering do not trigger false change alerts.
3. **Reset Transparency:** When a setting is restored to factory defaults using the Phase 2 reset button, the history log clearly records the change, showing the customized value it had previously and the factory default value it reverted to.

## 5. What Information Does Each History Record Contain?

Every item displayed in the Change History timeline provides a clear, comprehensive breakdown of the change:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SETTING CHANGE RECORD                                                                 │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Setting Name:       Pay Day (Monthly Salary Disbursement Date)                        │

│  Date & Time:        October 9, 2026 at 10:15 AM (UTC)                                 │
│  Updated By:         Asha Rao (HR Administrator)                                       │
│  Update Channel:     Settings Hub                                                      │
│  Business Reason:    Shifted cutoff to accommodate end-of-month banking processing      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  PREVIOUS VALUE:     28                                                                │
│  NEW VALUE:          30                                                                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Detailed Information Breakdown

* **Date & Timestamp:** The exact date and time when the change was successfully saved and applied.
* **Category:** The organizational settings category (e.g. *Payroll Calendar*, *Document Retention*, *Corporate Branding*, *Company Notifications*).
* **Setting Name:** The user-friendly title of the configuration rule that was modified.
* **Previous Value:** The setting value immediately prior to this update. If the setting was newly configured, this indicates *Unset / None*.
* **New Value:** The new value currently in effect.
* **Changed By:** The full name of the team member who initiated the save.
* **Role:** The administrative role held by the user at the time the change was executed (e.g. `HR Administrator`).
* **Business Reason:** The written justification provided when submitting the change. This is especially prominent for high-risk settings where a reason was mandated by Phase 2 shields.
* **Update Channel:** The interface through which the change was made:
  * `Settings Hub`: Saved through the central Settings Hub.
  * `Module Screen`: Saved through a dedicated domain screen (such as the Company Profile page).
  * `System Automation`: Applied automatically by a scheduled background process or automated workflow.

2. Confirming an operational warning dialog.

In Phase 3, these high-risk protections are fully integrated into the audit timeline:
* The business justification entered by the administrator is permanently affixed to the record.
* During audits, compliance officers can instantly inspect the rationale behind every critical policy adjustment without needing to request offline documentation.

## 8. User-Facing Validation & System Protections

The Change History dashboard incorporates built-in safeguards to ensure smooth navigation and data accuracy:

* **Chronological Integrity:** The timeline is strictly sorted from newest to oldest. You can always see the most recent corporate policy changes immediately upon opening the tab.
* **Date Sequence Guard:** If you set a date filter where the *End Date* precedes the *Start Date*, the system highlights the fields in red and prevents the search until the dates are corrected.
* **Category-Setting Alignment:** If you choose a specific setting that does not belong to your chosen category, the system alerts you and helps you align the filter criteria.
* **Pagination Bounds:** When reviewing large datasets, the system allows viewing between 1 and 100 records per page. The default view presents 50 clean, manageable entries.
* **Strict Tenant Privacy:** You will only ever see configuration history belonging to your own organization. Settings changes made by other companies hosted on the platform are completely invisible and strictly segregated.

## 9. Success & Failure Scenarios

### Successful Scenarios

#### Scenario A: Conducting a Routine Quarterly Compliance Review
1. An HR Administrator opens **Settings > Change History**.
2. She selects the **Date Range** filter for the previous quarter (e.g. July 1 to September 30).
3. She filters by the **Tax & Statutory** category.
4. The system instantly loads all modifications made to Provident Fund, ESI, and Professional Tax switches during that quarter.
5. She reviews the exact dates, the administrators who made the adjustments, and their confirmed notes, providing complete documentation for the financial auditor in minutes.

#### Scenario B: Investigating an Unexpected Notification Recipient Change
1. An invoice reminder email is sent to an unexpected email address.
2. The HR Administrator navigates to **Settings > Change History**.
3. She filters by the **Billing & Invoices** category and selects **Billing Notification Emails**.
4. The timeline displays a record from three weeks prior, showing that another HR administrator updated the notification email list, complete with their name and the exact timestamp.
5. The administrator can verify whether the update was intentional and update the contacts if needed.

---

### Failure Scenarios & Troubleshooting

#### Scenario 1: Access Denied / Locked Out of Change History
* **What Happens:** A People Manager attempts to open the Change History tab or navigate to the history link. The screen displays an **"Access Restricted — Administrator Privileges Required"** notification.
* **Why It Happened:** Department managers have view-only access to their specific approval authorities; they do not have authority to view company-wide policy change histories.
* **What to Do:** If audit access is required for a compliance role, the user's role must be upgraded to HR Administrator by an authorized company leader.

#### Scenario 2: Inverted Date Filter Warning
* **What Happens:** An administrator enters a *From* date of October 15 and a *To* date of October 10. Clicking *Apply Filters* displays the message: *"Invalid Date Range: The end date cannot be earlier than the start date."*
* **Why It Happened:** The search window is chronologically inverted.
* **What to Do:** Adjust the *To* date to be equal to or later than the *From* date and click *Apply Filters* again.

#### Scenario 3: Category and Setting Mismatch
* **What Happens:** An administrator selects the category *Payroll Calendar*, but types or selects the setting *Letter Retention Days*. The search displays: *"Filter Conflict: 'Letter Retention Days' is not part of the Payroll Calendar category."*
* **Why It Happened:** The specified setting belongs to the Document Management module, not Payroll.
* **What to Do:** Clear the Category filter to search for the setting across the entire company, or set the Category filter to *Document Retention*.

#### Scenario 4: Module Not Included in Organization's Plan
* **What Happens:** An administrator attempts to filter by a specialized module (e.g. advanced Document Management) that is not part of their current subscription tier. The system presents an informative message: *"This category is not available on your current subscription plan."*
* **Why It Happened:** Settings history respects your organization's active feature subscriptions.
* **What to Do:** To unlock settings and history for that module, contact your account executive to upgrade your subscription plan.

#### Scenario 5: Temporary Archive Maintenance Banner
* **What Happens:** The Change History tab displays records for Payroll and Document settings, but a banner at the top notes: *"Some historical document archive records are temporarily refreshing."*
* **Why It Happened:** One of the platform's underlying archival stores is temporarily performing maintenance or experiencing a momentary delay.
* **What to Do:** No action is needed. All remaining historical records are fully accessible. The banner will automatically disappear once the archive refresh completes.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: Who has access to view the Settings Change History?
**A:** Access is granted strictly to **HR Administrators**. People Managers, general employees, and platform infrastructure staff cannot view the change history.

#### Q2: Can a People Manager view the history of settings they can see?
**A:** No. While department managers can view the two approval governance categories (*Payroll Approval Authority* and *Document Approval Authority*), the historical audit ledger is reserved exclusively for HR Administrators to protect administrative privacy and compliance integrity.

#### Q3: Can an administrator delete or clear old history records?
**A:** No. The Settings Change History is a permanent, append-only audit ledger. No user or administrator can alter, edit, or delete historical records. This ensures complete non-repudiation and compliance with statutory audit standards.

#### Q4: If I open a category and click "Save" without changing anything, does it create an audit record?
**A:** No. The system uses intelligent change detection. If no values were modified, the save operation completes smoothly without creating unnecessary clutter in the audit history.

#### Q5: What happens when I reset a setting to its factory default?
**A:** The reset is fully recorded in the Change History! The record clearly identifies the customized value that was removed and shows the factory default value that took its place, marked with a *Factory Reset* badge.

#### Q6: Are changes made on the Company Profile page included in this history?
**A:** Yes! Modifications to company notification email contacts and reminder lead days are recorded in the Change History ledger regardless of whether they are saved via the Settings Hub or the Organization Profile page. The history record will indicate the channel where the change was made.

#### Q7: Why do some older history entries show "None" for the business reason?
**A:** In Phase 2, written business reasons were made mandatory specifically for high-risk settings (such as approval thresholds and retention policies). For standard operational settings (such as UI display preferences), entering a reason is optional. If no reason was entered during save, the field simply displays *None*.

#### Q8: How far back does the Change History go?
**A:** The Change History preserves all recorded configuration modifications indefinitely, starting from the day your organization began managing settings on the platform.

#### Q9: Can I filter history by a specific team member who left the company?
**A:** Yes. If a former HR Administrator made configuration changes during their tenure, their name and recorded role remain permanently associated with those changes, ensuring full accountability even after employee departure.

#### Q10: How does the Change History handle different timezones?
**A:** All timestamps are recorded with exact precision. The interface displays dates and times clearly, allowing team members across different regional offices to coordinate audits accurately.

#### Q11: What should I do if a search returns "Filter Conflict"?
**A:** A filter conflict occurs if you select a specific category and a setting that does not belong to that category (e.g. *Payroll Calendar* and *Document Retention Days*). Simply clear the Category dropdown or select the matching category to resolve the conflict.

#### Q12: Why don't I see history for a particular category on our dashboard?
**A:** Settings history respects your company's active subscription tier. If your organization's subscription plan does not include a specific module (such as advanced Document Management), that module's settings and history are hidden from your view.

#### Q13: Can I export or print the Change History report?
**A:** Yes. You can filter the history for the desired date range or category and use your browser's print or PDF export function to generate a clean, executive-ready compliance report.

#### Q14: How does Phase 3 affect the existing settings tabs in Payroll or Documents?
**A:** It does not affect them at all! The existing settings pages under *Payroll > Settings* and *Documents > Settings* continue to operate normally. Phase 3 simply provides a centralized historical view of all changes across every module.

#### Q15: What is the maximum number of history records I can view at once?
**A:** You can choose to display 25, 50, or up to 100 entries per page. You can easily navigate to earlier records using the pagination controls at the bottom of the list.

#### Q16: Will our historical records disappear if we change our subscription plan?
**A:** No. Your organization's historical audit records remain safely preserved in the database. If your subscription changes, your audit trail remains intact.
